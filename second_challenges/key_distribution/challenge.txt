Alice wants to send Bob a secret, but they’ve never met. Luckily, the company runs a public key directory: everyone registers their ElGamal public key under their name, and Alice simply looks up Bob’s entry before encrypting.

The directory is open for everyone to use. Alice is happy to send her secret to anyone listed in it. Can you read what she sends to Bob?

Target: http://cns-test.idlab.uantwerpen.be:50011

GET /params returns the public parameters p and g GET /directory lists all registered names and their public keys POST /directory with JSON {"name": ..., "y": ...} registers a public key GET /send?to=<name> makes Alice encrypt her secret to the key listed under <name>

The secret is encoded as m = int.from_bytes(secret, "big")
